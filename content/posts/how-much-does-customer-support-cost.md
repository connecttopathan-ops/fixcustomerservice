For most small businesses, customer support costs somewhere between a few thousand and a few tens of thousands of euros a month. The biggest share is people. Where your team sits in Europe changes the answer most. The same agent can cost five times more in one country than another.

This guide shows what goes into the number, gives real labour cost figures, and ends with a formula you can fill in with your own.

## What goes into the cost

| Cost | What it covers | Easy to miss? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People | Salaries of everyone answering customers, plus employer taxes and benefits | No |
| Management | The share of a manager's or owner's time spent on support | Yes |
| Software | Helpdesk, chat, phone system, surveys, chatbot | No |
| Messaging and calls | Per-message WhatsApp charges, phone minutes | Yes |
| Hiring and training | Recruiting, onboarding, the weeks before a new person is fully productive | Yes |
| Outsourcing | Invoices from an outside support team | No |

People usually make up the largest part by far. Software feels expensive when you buy it, but it is normally a small slice of the total.

## What one support person costs

The most useful public figure is Eurostat's hourly labour cost. It includes wages and employer costs such as social contributions. [In 2025 it averaged €34.9 an hour across the EU](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260331-2). It ranged from €12.0 in Bulgaria to €56.8 in Luxembourg.

Multiply by about 160 working hours and you get a rough monthly cost for one full-time person.

Support roles are often paid below the national average, so your real figure may be lower. But the spread between countries is the point. It is why moving support to a lower-cost country looks so attractive. It is also why that choice deserves care, because a lower hourly rate doesn't fix the reasons customers get in touch.

## What the software costs

Helpdesk software is usually priced per agent per month. As one example, [Zendesk's plans run from about $19 to $115 per agent a month](https://www.voiceflow.com/blog/zendesk-pricing), before add-ons and AI charges. Most competitors sit in a similar range. Always check the vendor's own pricing page, because prices change often.

Two costs are easy to overlook:

- **AI and chatbot charges.** Many tools now charge per automated answer, on top of the per-agent price.
- **WhatsApp messages.** From 1 October 2026, replies through the WhatsApp API are charged per message. See [WhatsApp pricing from 1 October 2026](/blog/whatsapp-pricing-october-2026).

## Three example teams

These are illustrations using round numbers: €3,500 a month per agent including employer costs, and software at €100 per agent a month. Swap in your own figures.

| Team | People | Software | Management time | Monthly total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner plus 1 agent | €3,500 | €100 | €1,000 of the owner's time | about €4,600 |
| 3 agents | €10,500 | €300 | €1,500 | about €12,300 |
| 8 agents and a team lead | €32,500 | €900 | included in team lead | about €33,400 |

In the last row, the team lead is counted at €4,500 a month and included under People.

The owner's time is the cost most small businesses forget. If you spend ten hours a week answering customers, that time has a price, even if nobody invoices you for it.

## The formula

Put your own numbers into this.

**Monthly support cost** = (people cost + management time + software + messaging and calls + outsourcing invoices) for one month

Then divide it by the number of contacts you handled that month. That gives your **cost per contact**, the number that tells you if support is getting more efficient as you grow. We walk through it step by step in [how to calculate cost per contact](/blog/how-to-calculate-cost-per-contact).

## Is your support too expensive?

The total alone doesn't tell you. A €30,000 team that handles 10,000 contacts costs €3 per contact. A €12,000 team that handles 2,000 costs €6. The bigger team is the more efficient one.

What matters is the trend. If your cost per contact stays flat or rises as you grow, every new customer costs you more to support. If it falls, your setup is getting better, not just bigger.

If yours is rising, start with [how to reduce customer support costs](/blog/how-to-reduce-customer-support-costs). Or our [customer service audit](/customer-service-audit) can work out your real figure and what is driving it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a customer support agent cost per month in Europe?

It depends heavily on the country. Based on Eurostat's 2025 hourly labour costs, one full-time employee costs about €1,920 a month in Bulgaria. It is €5,584 on the EU average and €9,088 in Luxembourg. Support roles are often paid below these averages.

### What is the biggest cost in customer support?

People. Salaries and employer costs are usually by far the largest share, well ahead of software.

### How much does helpdesk software cost?

Usually priced per agent per month. Zendesk, for example, runs from about $19 to $115 per agent a month before add-ons. AI features are often charged separately.

### How do I know if my support costs are too high?

Work out your cost per contact each month. If it rises or stays flat as you grow, your support is getting more expensive per customer, and it is worth finding out why.
