03 · The rest of the damage

It doesn't stop at lost sales.

Money that never comes in

  • Customers leave without complaining. Most never tell you. They just stop buying. It looks like a marketing problem, but it started in support.
  • Enquiries die before they're sales. A slow answer to "how much?" loses the customer to whoever replied first.
  • People buy less often after one bad experience, and referrals stop.

Money going out that shouldn't

  • Bad support creates its own work. One issue handled badly becomes three more conversations about the same thing.
  • Hiring that doesn't help. You add people to cope with volume that better process would have removed, and that cost stays forever.
  • Refunds and goodwill discounts patching problems that shouldn't have happened.

Reputation and being found online

  • Google reviews decide it before you do. People check your rating before they buy or subscribe, and most bad reviews are about service, not the product.
  • Your search position follows your rating. Score, number of reviews and how recent they are all feed local search. Poor service pushes you down the map results, so fewer people find you at all.
  • You pay to undo it, with ad spend making up for the customers who read the reviews and left.

Your team and your business

  • Good people burn out and leave, and you pay to hire and train all over again.
  • Nobody learns anything. Without knowing what customers contact about, the business keeps breaking the same thing.
  • Growth feels risky instead of good, because more customers means more chaos.

09 · By size

It breaks differently at every size.

A few hundredmessages a month

You or one staff member answers everything between other jobs. There's no record of what came in, and evenings are silent.

Fix: a shared inbox, written answers for the top questions, and one person who owns it. The cheapest fix you'll ever make.

A few thousandmessages a month

You've hired two or three people. Quality depends on who picks up, nobody can say what customers are contacting about, and complaints still reach you directly.

Fix: proper tools, a way of tagging what comes in, reply-time targets, and a clear path for complaints.

Tens of thousandsmessages a month

Every time customers grow, headcount grows with them. Staff keep leaving. The same problems generate messages month after month and nobody outside support owns them.

Fix: stop the repeat questions at the source, plan staffing properly, and get the rest of the business fixing what causes contacts.

Hundreds of thousandsmessages a month

Support is one of your biggest costs. You may have an outsourced partner hitting their targets while customers get unhappier, and automation bought before anyone understood what to automate.

Fix: structural work. Self-service that actually works, proper management of outsourced teams, and automation aimed at the right questions.

These are the numbers we measure and move. Most businesses track none of them, which is why support problems stay invisible until they show up in the revenue figures.

First reply timeHow long a customer waits for any human responseSales go to whoever answered first. Slow replies lose ready buyers before you know they existed.

15 · Questions

What people ask first.

What does it cost?

Fixed price, agreed before anything starts. Never hourly, never a surprise. The size depends on how big your operation is and how much of the work your own team takes on. We'll give you a straight number on the free call, and the first look is small enough that most businesses treat it as a test.

We're a small business. Is this for us?

Small businesses usually get the biggest improvement for the least money, because nothing has been set up yet. Often the one-week look is all you need, and your own team does the rest.

We get a huge number of messages. Can you handle that?

That's the environment behind thirteen years of this work. Large operations have their own problems: outsourced teams, automation, staffing models. Those are the familiar ones.

Are you going to tell us to buy a chatbot?

Only if your questions actually suit one. Plenty of businesses have been sold automation that answers the wrong things. We'll give you a straight view on what to automate and what to keep human.

Will you replace our team with people overseas?

Only if that's right for your margins, languages and customers, and only if you want to. Often the answer is keeping your team and giving them a better setup.

How much attention do we get?

We take on a small number of clients at once, deliberately. You'll have a direct number, and you'll always know what's being worked on that week.

What if we don't have any numbers?

Most businesses don't. Establishing them is the first thing we do, and it's included.

Do you work outside the UAE?

Yes. Almost all of the work is remote, so your location makes no difference. Clients outside the Gulf are welcome, and in-person sessions are easy anywhere in the UAE.
