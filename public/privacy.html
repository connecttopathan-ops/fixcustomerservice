Privacy policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

This site is run by Abdullah Khan, trading as Fix Customer Service, based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This page explains what happens to information you send through the site.

What is collected

Only what you type into the contact form or send by WhatsApp. That is usually your name, your business name, and a phone number or email address, plus a rough indication of how many customer messages your business handles.

The calculator on the site runs entirely in your browser. The figures you enter are never sent anywhere and are not stored.

How it is used

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call
  • To prepare for that call, so the conversation is useful
  • Nothing else

Your details are not sold, rented or shared with third parties. You will not be added to a mailing list, and you will not receive marketing you did not ask for.

Where it is stored

Contact form submissions are delivered by email through a form service and kept in an email inbox. WhatsApp messages stay in WhatsApp. Enquiry details are kept for as long as there is an active conversation, and deleted on request.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers and Google receives your IP address as part of that request.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs for security and performance reasons.

Advertising

If you arrived here from an advertisement on Google, Meta or LinkedIn, that platform may have recorded the click on its own systems under its own privacy terms. No advertising or tracking scripts run on this site.

Your rights

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Requests are handled within thirty days.

Client information

If we work together, a separate non-disclosure agreement governs your business data. Access to your systems is read-only wherever possible, no customer data is copied off your systems, and access ends when the work does.

Contact

For anything on this page, get in touch through the contact section of the site.
